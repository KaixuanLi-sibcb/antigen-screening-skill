# Glossary

## Antigen Accessibility

The evidence-backed assessment that a candidate has an extracellular, secreted, membrane-accessible, or otherwise modality-reachable region. It cannot be inferred from gene symbol alone.

## ECD Readiness

The practical readiness of an extracellular domain for screening-material design. It considers ECD boundaries, length, topology, isoform ambiguity, glycosylation, cysteine/disulfide risk, and display fallback needs.

## Constructability

The feasibility of producing a useful antigen material, such as soluble ECD, Fc-dimer, AviTag-biotin multimer, full-length cell display, VLP/nanodisc display, or a domain fragment.

## Evidence Provenance

The traceable record of where an evidence claim came from, how it was retrieved or computed, what version or hash identifies the source, and whether the value is confirmed, inferred, missing, conflicted, not applicable, or errored.

## Local Fixture Validation

A local regression process that uses privacy-safe fixture inputs and generated output metrics to verify provenance and planning behavior. It is not a final biological target validation claim.

## Fixture Smoke Test

A privacy-safe test path that uses small local fixtures to validate the workflow from a clean checkout without private data or live network access.

## Screening Strategy

A planning artifact that recommends antigen formats for antibody discovery and binding studies based on readiness and constructability evidence.

## Validation Ladder

A staged experimental plan for confirming binder specificity and target dependence through orthogonal, genetic, independent-reagent, tagged-protein, cell-binding, loss-of-binding, gain-of-binding, and cross-species checks.
