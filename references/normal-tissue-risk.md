# Normal Tissue Risk

## Risk Classes

- `low`: restricted normal expression or manageable modality context.
- `medium`: lineage or compartment expression that may be acceptable only with mitigation.
- `high`: critical organ, broad normal tissue, high plasma abundance, endothelium, hematopoietic progenitor, or essential immune compartment risk.
- `unknown`: no reliable normal tissue evidence.

## High-Risk Contexts

Critical tissues include heart, CNS, lung epithelium, kidney, liver, endothelium, hematopoietic progenitors, and essential immune compartments.

Risk interpretation is modality-specific. A lineage antigen may be acceptable for a hematologic CAR context but not for broad solid-tumor targeting.
