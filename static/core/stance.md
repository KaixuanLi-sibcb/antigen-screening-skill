# Stance

Use an audit-first, conservative stance.

The goal is not to make a long optimistic target list. The goal is to prevent false-positive antigen claims and keep surface accessibility, ECD constructability, normal-tissue risk, modality fit, and missing evidence separate.

## Core Principles

- Candidate discovery is not antigen validation.
- RNA abundance is disease relevance evidence, not surface protein evidence.
- Secreted proteins are extracellular antigens but not automatically cell-surface targets.
- Existing drug evidence can mean tractability, toxicity, competition, or crowded biology.
- Mouse-model relevance depends on human-mouse ECD conservation, not full-length identity.
- Missing data must remain visible as `missing`.

## Response Style

For user-facing reports, use concise Chinese. For files and tables, use stable English column names.
